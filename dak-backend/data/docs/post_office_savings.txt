INDIA POST SMALL SAVINGS, INVESTMENT & LIFE INSURANCE SCHEMES OFFICIAL GUIDE

1. Small Savings & Fixed-Term Schemes:
- Post Office Savings Account (SB):
  * Interest Rate: 4.0% per annum (calculated on daily balance and credited annually).
  * Minimum Deposit: ₹500 to open, minimum ongoing balance ₹500.
  * Account Types: Single, Joint (Up to 3 adults), or Minor through guardian.
  * Tax Status: Interest tax-free up to ₹10,000 under Section 80TTA.

- Post Office Time Deposit (POTD):
  * Tenure Options: 1 Year (6.9%), 2 Years (7.0%), 3 Years (7.1%), 5 Years (7.5%).
  * Compounding: Compounded quarterly and paid annually.
  * Tax Benefit: 5-Year Time Deposit qualifies for deduction under Section 80C.

- Post Office Recurring Deposit (PORD):
  * Interest Rate: 6.7% per annum compounded quarterly.
  * Tenure: 5 Years (60 monthly deposits). Minimum deposit ₹100/month.
  * Tax Status: Interest is fully taxable.

- National Savings Certificate (NSC VIII Issue):
  * Interest Rate: 7.7% per annum compounded annually, payable on maturity.
  * Tenure: 5 Years. Minimum deposit ₹1,000, no upper limit.
  * Tax Status: Deposit & reinvested interest eligible for Section 80C deduction.

- Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP):
  * Interest Rate: 7.5% per annum compounded annually.
  * Tenure: 115 Months (9 Years & 7 Months).
  * Key Feature: Guaranteed doubling of invested amount.
  * Tax Status: Taxable interest; no Section 80C deduction.

2. Regular Income & Long-Term Wealth Schemes:
- Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS):
  * Interest Rate: 7.4% per annum paid monthly into savings account.
  * Tenure: 5 Years.
  * Investment Limits: Up to ₹9 Lakh for single accounts, up to ₹15 Lakh for joint accounts.

- Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS):
  * Interest Rate: 8.2% per annum, paid quarterly (April 1, July 1, October 1, January 1).
  * Tenure: 5 Years (extendable in blocks of 3 years).
  * Eligibility: Individuals aged 60+ (or 55+ for voluntary retired / defense personnel).
  * Maximum Investment: ₹30 Lakh. Qualifies for Section 80C deduction.

- Public Provident Fund (PPF):
  * Interest Rate: 7.1% per annum compounded annually.
  * Tenure: 15 Years (extendable in 5-year blocks).
  * Deposit Limits: Minimum ₹500/year, Maximum ₹1.5 Lakh/year.
  * Tax Status: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) — Tax rebate on deposits under 80C, tax-free interest, and tax-free maturity.

3. Dedicated Demographic Schemes:
- Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY / SSA):
  * Interest Rate: 8.2% per annum compounded annually.
  * Target: Girl child under 10 years of age (max 2 accounts per family).
  * Tenure: 21 Years from account opening (or upon marriage after attaining 18 years). Deposits made for first 15 years.
  * Deposit Limits: Minimum ₹250/year, Maximum ₹1.5 Lakh/year.
  * Tax Status: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) under Section 80C.

- Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC):
  * Interest Rate: 7.5% per annum compounded quarterly.
  * Target: Exclusively for women and girls.
  * Tenure: 2 Years.
  * Maximum Deposit: ₹2 Lakh (Minimum ₹1,000).

4. Postal Life Insurance & Social Security Schemes:
- Postal Life Insurance (PLI):
  * Target: Central/State government employees, defense personnel, PSU/bank employees, and professionals.
  * Variants: Suraksha (Whole Life), Santosh (Endowment Assurance), Suvidha (Convertible Whole Life), Yugal Suraksha (Joint Life), Sumangal (Anticipated Endowment).
  * High bonus rates and lowest premium rates in the market.

- Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI):
  * Target: Designed specifically for the rural population across India.
  * Variants: Gram Suraksha, Gram Santosh, Gram Suvidha, Gram Sumangal, Bal Jeevan Bima (Children plan).

- Social Security Schemes:
  * Atal Pension Yojana (APY): Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 for unorganized sector (age 18-40).
  * Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY): ₹2 Lakh life insurance cover at ₹436/year.
  * Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY): ₹2 Lakh accidental insurance cover at ₹20/year.
